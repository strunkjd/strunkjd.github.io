Privacy policy

Aurora is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

Only the owner's Gmail, with Google's read-only permission (gmail.readonly), and only after the owner signs in and agrees. It cannot send, delete, label or change email.

How the data is used

When the owner asks about their email, recent messages are read and summarized for the owner. Email content is processed on the owner's computer and is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and is not used to train AI models. Aurora's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
